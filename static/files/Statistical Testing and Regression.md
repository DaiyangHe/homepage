Tests of proportion (2 sided): $H_0: p=p_0; ~H_1:p\neq p_0$. when $n\to \infty$, $z=\frac{x-np_{0}}{\sqrt{np_{0}q_{0}}}=\frac{\hat{p}-p_{0}}{\sqrt{p_{0}q_{0}/n}}$. For 2 sided critical region: $z<-z_{\alpha/2}$ and $z>z_{\alpha/2}$. For two samples of size $n_{1}$ and $n_{2}$, when large, $Z=\frac{(\widehat{P}_1-\widehat{P}_2)-(p_1-p_2)}{\sqrt{p_1q_1/n_1+p_2q_2/n_2}}$. To test $H_0: p_{1}=p_{2}$, $Z=\frac{\widehat{P}_1-\widehat{P}_2}{\sqrt{pq(1/n_1+1/n_2)}}$, where $p=\frac{x_{1}+x_{2}}{n_{1}+n_{2}}$. Tests of variance (one sample): If the population is normally distributed, $\chi^2=\frac{(n-1)s^2}{\sigma_0^2},\ df=n-1$. One tail problem: $\mathrm{~For~H_1:~\sigma^2>\sigma_0^2,~reject~H_0~if}\ \chi_{n-1}^2>\chi_{n-1,\alpha}^2$; $\mathrm{~For~H}_1{:}\sigma^2<\sigma_0^2,\mathrm{~reject~H}_0\mathrm{~if~}\ \chi_{n-1}^2<\chi_{n-1,1-\alpha}^2$. two samples: $f=\frac{s_1^2}{s_2^2}$, $df=n_{1}-1,n_{2}-1$. $H_0:\sigma_{1}=\sigma_{2}$, critical region：$f<f_{1-\alpha}(v_1,v_2)$ ($H_1: \sigma_{1}<\sigma_{2}$); $f>f_{\alpha}(v_{1},v_{2})$ ($H_1: \sigma_{1}>\sigma_{2}$); $f<f_{1-\alpha/2}(v_{1},v_{2})\mathrm{~or~}f>f_{\alpha/2}(v_{1},v_{2})$ ($H_1: \sigma_{1}\neq\sigma_{2}$). Simple linear regression model: $Y=\beta_0+\beta_1x+\epsilon$, $E(\epsilon)=0, Var(\epsilon)=\sigma^2$. Least square: minimize $SSE=\sum_{i=1}^ne_i^2=\sum_{i=1}^n(y_i-\hat{y_i})^2$. $b_1=\frac{n\sum_{i=1}^nx_iy_i-\left(\sum_{i=1}^nx_i\right)\left(\sum_{i=1}^ny_i\right)}{n\sum_{i=1}^nx_i^2-\left(\sum_{i=1}^nx_i\right)^2}=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^n(x_i-\bar{x})(y_i-\bar{y})}{\sum_{i=1}^n(x_i-\bar{x})^2}$, $b_0=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^ny_i-b_1\sum_{i=1}^nx_i}{n}=\bar{y}-b_1\bar{x}$. $S_{xx}=\sum_{i=1}^{n}(x_{i}-\bar{x})^{2},$$S_{yy}=\sum_{i=1}^{n}(y_{i}-\bar{y})^{2},$$S_{xy}=\sum_{i=1}^{n}(x_{i}-\bar{x})(y_{i}-\bar{y}).$ $SSE=S_{y}-b_{1}S_{xx}$. Unbiased estimate of $\sigma^2$: $s^2=\frac{SSE}{n-2}$.
